Structure
  • Steel – Jindal or equivalent
  • Cement – Dalmia, Bharathi or equivalent, 43 or 53 grade
  • Aggregates – 20mm & 40mm
  • Blocks – Standard solid concrete blocks (6 inch & 4 inch)
  • RCC Design Mix – M20 / M25 or as per structural designer recommendation
  • Ceiling Height – 10 feet (Finished Floor level to Finished Floor level)
Kitchen
  • Ceramic Wall Dado – Up to ₹60 per sq.ft.
  • Main Sink Faucet – Up to ₹2,000
  • Any other faucet or accessories – ISI Marked
  • Kitchen Sink – Stainless Steel single sink, make worth ₹6,000
Bathroom
  • Ceramic Wall Dado up to 7 feet height – Up to ₹60 per sq.ft.
  • Sanitarywares & CP fittings up to ₹50,000 per 1,000 sq.ft. of Hindware or Parryware make
  • CPVC Pipe – APL Apollo or equivalent
  • Bathroom doors – Waterproof flush doors or WPC
Doors & Windows
  • Windows – UPVC windows with glass and mesh shutters (3 track with 1 mesh), Lesso Eiti or equivalent
  • Main Door – Teak door with teak frame (5 inch × 3 inch), worth ₹30,000 including fixtures
  • Internal Doors – Membrane doors / flush door with laminates up to ₹11,000 including fixtures; Sal Wood frames (4 inch × 2.5 inch)
Painting
  • Interior Painting – JK Putty + Tractor Shyne Emulsion or equivalent
  • Exterior Painting – Asian Primer + Apex Exterior Emulsion Paint or equivalent
Flooring
  • Living & Dining Flooring – Tiles or Granite up to ₹100 per sq.ft.
  • Rooms & Kitchen Flooring – Tiles up to ₹80 per sq.ft.
  • Balcony and Open Areas – Anti-skid tiles up to ₹60 per sq.ft.
  • Staircase Flooring – Sadarahalli Granite up to ₹80 per sq.ft.
  • Parking Tiles – Anti-skid tiles up to ₹50 per sq.ft.
Electrical
  • Fire-proof wiring – Finolex Silver FR or equivalent
  • Switches & Sockets – Legrand Lyncus or Schneider Liva or equivalent
  • UPS Wiring Provision
Miscellaneous
  • Overhead Tank – Double-layered 1,500 Ltr Apollo tank
  • Underground Sump – 6,000 Ltrs
  • Staircase Railing – MS Railing
  • Window Grills – Basic MS Grill with enamel paint (reference rate ₹195 per sq.ft.)

ⓘ Important Guidelines & Disclaimers

  • Rates shown are reference baseline estimates for construction within Bhopal municipal regions. Outer suburbs may carry an additional 5% logistics markup.
  • Final drawings and structural design will follow applicable IS code requirements and the structural designer's recommendations.
  • Basement construction cost may range from ₹1,000 to ₹1,500 per sq.ft., depending on waterproofing grade and excavation depth.
  • GST of 18% is stated as applicable over and above the estimated built-up construction cost parameters; confirm current tax applicability in the final quotation.
  • Final cost depends on design, site conditions, selected materials, specifications and project requirements.
